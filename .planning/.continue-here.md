## What the close-out reviews found

Four defects, three of them security-relevant. Every one was a rule that had
been fixed at an **instance** and left open at its **class**, so each fix is
paired with a guard that closes the class.

1. **Three filtered routes returned rows of projects the caller cannot read** —
   `work_orders/list`, `reports/list` and `evidence/list`, the last of these the
   trusted audit trail. Probed against a running Companion with a principal
   holding no membership anywhere. Same class as the `alarms/list` leak of
   `9f53bcb`. Fixed in `c487f0b`;
   `tests/components/glt_flow_card/test_filtered_route_authority.py` derives its
   route list from `COMMAND_POLICY_CONTRACT` and asserts **rows** rather than
   response codes, because `test_policy_enumeration.py` asserts codes and for a
   filtered route success *is* the right code however many rows arrive with it.
2. **The SSRF destination re-check was correct only by memory.** Guarded in
   `b9727ed` by `test_outbound_destination_guard.py`, an AST sweep with an empty
   exemption list, mirroring what Phase 8 built for service dispatch.
3. **`verify-i18n-coverage` printed PASS over eleven German labels**, and
   T10-03 had been closed on that PASS. Its test was **linguistic** — `PROSE`
   needs two whitespace-separated words, `GERMAN` needs an umlaut — and
   `Projektname`, `Layername`, `Aufgabe` have neither. A second **structural**
   sweep now asks where a literal *went*: anything handed to `prompt`,
   `confirm`, `alert`, `.textContent` or `.innerText` is a finding whatever it
   looks like. Mutation-checked against the exact blind spot.
4. **The authored v040 parts did not parse as JavaScript.** `part06` carried a
   ternary whose `?` reads as optional chaining. `v040.test.mjs` proved each
   module was *mentioned* and never asked node to read the result; it now runs
   `node --check`.
