# 10-12 — What was never exercised, said out loud

**Status:** complete. Closes T10-17.

Four capabilities are published as *not exercised*, each with its reason,
because a reader who is not told assumes they were:

- **Screen-reader behaviour.** No assistive technology can be driven here. No
  test asserts that something "would be announced correctly" — that is asserting
  a belief.
- **Representative capacity.** A shared cloud container with no declared CPU
  allocation. The numbers support "the scenario is bounded and runs".
- **The composed release leaf.** No Docker engine, the same limitation eight
  phases have recorded with exact output.
- **Release provenance.** All five third-party repository endpoints answer 403
  for this session; `api.github.com/rate_limit` answers 200, so it is a
  repository-scope limit rather than a network one. Attaching five third-party
  repositories with credentials to satisfy a provenance check would be a
  disproportionate permission change.

This phase is the registry's first subject, and that is the right one: a
registry whose author exempted their own phase would be worth nothing.
