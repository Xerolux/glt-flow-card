# 10-06 — The two runtimes say the same thing, not merely mean it

Closes T10-06.

The existing parity gates compare **codes** — `site_vocabulary`,
`dispatch_vocabulary`, `period_vocabulary` fingerprints. They are the right
checks and they do not cover wording, so the Companion and the browser can drift
in what they *say* while agreeing on what they *mean*. A German operator reading
a Companion error and the browser's rendering of the same condition sees two
different sentences and reasonably concludes they are two different conditions.

Canonical bytes, for the reason recorded four times now.

## Steps

1. `catalog.py` mirroring the browser catalogs, sorted, with the same keys.
2. A canonicalization identical on both sides: sorted keys, no separators
   surprise, numbers as themselves.
3. `test/catalog-parity.test.mjs` comparing the two serializations.

## Acceptance

Changing one German sentence on one side fails, naming the key. Adding a key on
one side fails. The canonicalization is shared, not duplicated.
