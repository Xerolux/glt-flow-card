# 10-13 — Budgets, and what a number is allowed to mean

**Status:** complete. Closes T10-13 and T10-14.

Budgets per scenario and dimension, so a regression that doubles render time
fails instead of passing quietly. Generous where they were measured here and
marked as such — a tight budget derived from an unrepresentative machine is a
flaky test wearing a budget's clothes. The median is compared rather than the
slowest, because a shared container's slowest sample is a statement about the
neighbours.

**The registry decides what a number may claim, from its fingerprint.** An
unmarked environment supports "this scenario is bounded and runs"; only one
marked representative supports "the platform handles N objects". A number is
never promoted by being copied into a summary.

This is Phase 9's rule made mechanical: the shape of the cost is proven, the
magnitude is not claimed.
