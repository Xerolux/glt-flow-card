# 10-13 — Budgets, and what a number is allowed to mean

Closes T10-13 and T10-14.

Budgets recorded per scenario and per dimension, so a regression that doubles
render time fails instead of passing quietly. The budgets are generous where
they were measured here and marked as such — a tight budget derived from an
unrepresentative machine is a flaky test wearing a budget's clothes.

**The registry decides what a number may claim, from its fingerprint**: an
unmarked environment supports "this scenario is bounded and runs"; only an
environment marked representative supports "the platform handles N objects". A
number is never promoted by being copied into a summary.

This is Phase 9's rule made mechanical — the shape of the cost is proven, the
magnitude is not claimed.

## Acceptance

Exceeding a budget fails, naming the scenario, dimension, budget and measurement.
A measurement with no fingerprint is refused. A capacity claim citing an
unrepresentative measurement fails the registry.
