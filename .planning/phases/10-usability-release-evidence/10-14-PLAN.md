# 10-14 — Documentation that can be checked

Three new wiki pages, and the READMEs' claims brought under the registry.

**Accessibility.** What is asserted and by what; that automated checks decide a
minority of WCAG criteria; that the manual half is *not performed* in this
environment; and that a `title` is not a name — the defect the product shipped.

**Localization.** Two complete catalogs, a lookup that refuses rather than falls
back, plurals as data, and what adding a third locale actually requires. Also
the honest note that RTL *readiness* is proven and no RTL locale ships.

**Release Evidence.** What the registry is, why a failed claim is published as
failed, and the four capabilities never exercised here.

Every claim-shaped sentence in both READMEs either cites registry evidence or
stops being a claim. Starting with "`test/` – lightweight validation tests",
which has been false for some time.

## Acceptance

`node tools/verify-docs-site.mjs` passes with the new sources. A claim in a
README that is not in the registry fails the registry test.
