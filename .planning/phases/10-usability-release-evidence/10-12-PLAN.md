# 10-12 — What was never exercised, said out loud

Closes T10-17.

The release evidence has to state which capabilities were never exercised here,
or a reader assumes they were. Four are known before the work starts, and each
becomes a **published** registry entry rather than an omission:

- **Screen-reader behaviour.** No assistive technology can be driven here. The
  manual half is recorded as *not performed*. No test will assert that something
  "would be announced correctly" — that is asserting a belief.
- **Representative capacity.** A shared cloud container with no declared CPU
  allocation. Numbers are recorded with that fingerprint and support "the
  scenario is bounded and runs", never "the platform handles N".
- **The composed release leaf.** No Docker engine, the same limitation eight
  phases have recorded with exact output.
- **Release provenance (F-01).** All five third-party repository endpoints
  answer 403 for this session. `api.github.com/rate_limit` answers 200, so this
  is a repository-scope limit rather than a network one. Attaching five
  third-party repositories with credentials to satisfy a provenance check would
  be a disproportionate permission change and was not made.

This phase is the registry's first subject, and it is the right one: a registry
whose author exempted their own phase would be worth nothing.

## Acceptance

Each of the four appears in the registry output with its reason. Marking any of
them `pass` without evidence fails the build.
