# 10-04 is the sweep; this is the proof that the sweep can see

Closes T10-02.

Accent-and-pad — `Ëxämplé Téxt » ` — stays readable while being unmistakably not
the source string. **Generated from the catalogs at test time**, because a
checked-in pseudo-locale drifts from what it is testing and then tests nothing.

Three defects it catches, and the reason it is worth more than a key count:

- a **missing key**, because the fallback shows through as un-accented text;
- a **hardcoded string**, because everything localized changes shape and
  anything that does not is not going through the catalog;
- a **layout that assumes German length**, because the padding expands further
  than German already does.

## Steps

1. `tools/pseudo-locale.mjs`: derive from `catalog-en.mjs`, deterministic.
2. `test/pseudo-locale.test.mjs`: render each surface under the pseudo-locale
   and assert every visible string is accented.
3. A mutation case: reintroduce a `?? COPY.en[key]` fallback in one module and
   assert the test fails.

## Acceptance

The mutation case is committed as a comment naming what was tried and what
failed, not as a disabled test. Un-accented text in the output fails with the
string that was not localized.
