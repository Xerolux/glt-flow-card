# 07-19 — Prove the measured values are reached, and bound the cost

**Status:** complete. 455 Node, 510 Python, 61 exact-dist browser tests pass.

## What this plan was for

Phase 6 shipped a defect one layer out from where its own register drew the
line. Retiring the four alarm derivations left `activeAlarm` reading
`card._alarmState`, and only `alarmsPanel` ever wrote it — so the toolbar
badge, the per-site active count and the report's Status column all reported
*no active alarms* until an operator happened to open the modal.
`test/shipped-alarm-truth.test.mjs` passed the whole time, because
`alarms/list` does appear in the shipped bytes, in the one place nothing else
reached.

**Phase 7 had built the identical shape**, and 07-18 confirmed it: the trend
surfaces render what they are given, and only the trends panel gave them
anything. Worse, `_seriesFor` still called the retired `aggregateSeries`, so a
plain render put an epoch-aligned `Math.floor(x / bucketMs)` bucket on the
screen — displaced by an hour or two year-round, containing the wrong hour on a
transition day, and unable to express a month at all while the report designer
offers months and years.

## What changed

`_seriesFor` returns the Companion's measured series where there is one.
`refreshHistoryState` fetches it on plain render, throttled at 60 s, stamping
*before* the request so a Companion that is refusing or unreachable is asked
once per interval rather than once per render. 07-09 bounded the backend's
query cost precisely so the browser would not hand it back.

## Two assertions, both mutation-verified

**Render the card, open nothing, read the number.** A grep cannot tell
reachable from reached. Removing `refreshHistoryState(this)` from `_render`
fails it with *"the card rendered without ever asking the Companion for
measured history"*. The coverage assertion names the confident zero directly:
a surface reporting 0 % coverage and no gaps looks exactly like a plant with no
data, and nobody investigates a zero.

**Ten renders produce exactly one Recorder query** — not "at most one". This
was written as `toBeLessThanOrEqual(1)` first, which a card that never fetched
would satisfy while failing at the very thing the bound protects. That is the
vacuous pass this suite already corrected once in Phase 4, so it is now
`toBe(1)`. Removing the throttle produces **23** queries — more than the ten
renders, because the card re-renders on its own as well, which makes the point
better than the ten would have.

## A retirement got stronger

`aggregateSeries` moved from `RETIRED_IN_ARTIFACT` to `REMOVED_BY_BUNDLER`, and
the move is the finding rather than bookkeeping. It was "reachable and inert"
only because `_seriesFor` still called it. That call was its last reference, so
esbuild now drops it: **the artifact cannot display an epoch-aligned bucket at
all, rather than being trusted not to.** Its replacement is proven by behaviour
in `test_period_resolution.py`, against a corpus generated by executing the
vendored Home Assistant — which is where the 23-hour day, the 25-hour day and
the 745-hour month are actually established.

## Still open, carried from 07-18

`history/coverage` and `history/export` remain shells returning stated
`unavailable`. Coverage needs the period's expected instants, whose bucket step
is a decision wanting a corpus behind it; inferring it from returned rows is
the defect `expected_instants` exists to prevent. **07-20 must not mark T7-nn
rows that depend on those two routes as verified.**
