**Phase 5 is complete.** 20 of 20 plans implemented, 15 of 16 threats verified.
Read `05-SUMMARY.md` first; it records what the phase found as well as what it
built.

## What is open, and why

- **T5-16** stays `planned`. Its owner is `npm run test:phase5:release`, which
  needs a Docker engine. T2-16, T3-14 and T4-14 are unmarked for the same
  reason. Do **not** mark any of them from a green `ha-artifacts`: that is one
  of the leaf's four parts, and a composed leaf verified from its parts is a
  leaf nobody composed.
- **Two diagonals in a closed box** cannot be separated by a lane offset;
  resolving it needs a jog. Reported in `spacing_violations`, documented in
  `docs/wiki/Symbols-Routing.md`.
- **Five `prompt()` calls** remain on editor naming paths.
- **v040 extension parts 05 and 06 are not in the shipped artifact.** They are
  the authored form of an extension `apply-v040.yml` bundles, and that workflow
  was never run after Phase 4. The two paths that mattered were fixed directly
  in `src/generated-bases/glt-flow-card.base.js`; the mechanism is untouched,
  and part 06's symbol renderer is still missing from what ships. Re-bundling
  all seven parts also resets the package version to 0.4.0, so it is a build
  decision, not a phase task.

## Traps this phase hit, all of them twice-costly

1. **Build after committing source, then commit the manifest, then re-run the
   gates.** The manifest records the last commit that touched a canonical
   source; built beforehand it names the parent and carries `dirty: true`.
2. **Three packaging lists.** The stager, the independent validator and the
   staging test each name the Companion package's contents separately, and that
   is deliberate. Adding a module means saying it three times.
3. **Two policy tables.** A route is declared in the shipped `policy.py` *and*
   the test-owned `policy_contract.py`.
4. **Every websocket request field needs a default.** The generic policy prober
   sends a probe carrying only `type`, and a required field turns a policy
   decision into a schema rejection.
5. **Refusal codes must come from `ERROR_CODES`.** A new top-level code widens
   the contract; put the detail in the message body.
6. **A gate generated from the previous phase's carries its predecessor's
   numbers.** The roadmap slice bounds, the plan regex, the threat count and
   the leaf-owner id all needed correcting, and an uncorrected slice yields a
   gate that binds nothing and passes.
7. **A threat's owner command must actually match something.** T5-03's grep
   group did not exist until the catalog assertions were moved into their own
   test.
