# 03-14 — One precedence table, sixteen inputs, one state

> **Reconstructed at close-out**, from this plan, `03-SUMMARY.md` and the code
> at head — not written at execution time. It records what shipped and what the
> plan asked for; it is not a contemporaneous account of the work.

**Status:** complete. Closes T3-11 and T3-12.

A fixed table resolves the sixteen inputs to exactly one state. Written as a
table rather than as nested conditions, because the pair that inverts is the one
nobody writes a condition for.

**Trust outranks activity.** A communication error, an invalid value or a stale
reading is **never** `running`: a plant that has stopped talking is not a plant
that is running, and the comfortable reading is the dangerous one.

Symbol, quality, freshness, the German and English label and the drill-down are
all projections of one resolved value — five renderings of one decision rather
than five decisions that agree today.
