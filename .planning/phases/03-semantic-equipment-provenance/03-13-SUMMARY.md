**Status:** complete. Closes T3-10.

**Nothing binds without an explicit acceptance carrying the previewed diff's
identity.** Accepting a preview that has since changed is accepting something
nobody looked at, so the acceptance names *which* preview it accepts.

**An acceptance is undoable as one batch.** Mapping is where a plausible-looking
mistake affects dozens of points at once, and undoing it point by point is not
undo.
