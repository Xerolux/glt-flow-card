**Status:** complete. Closes T3-13.

The semantic tree, provenance card, mapping review and state badge are asserted
in the **generated artifact**, not in `src/` — the rule every UI phase has
followed since.

**Every state and health value carries a non-colour cue**, in German and
English. A state distinguished only by a tint is no information at all on a
monochrome kiosk or in forced colours.

**No unauthorized entity, device or area data reaches any browser sink.** The
surface renders what the server decided and derives nothing of its own.
