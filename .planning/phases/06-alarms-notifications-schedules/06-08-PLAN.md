## Phase Goal

**As an** operator, **I want** a Home Assistant restart to be invisible to my acknowledged alarms, **so that** a maintenance window does not page everybody at once.

<objective>Make the lifecycle restart-safe.</objective>

<tasks>
<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: Distinguish unavailable from cleared</name>
  <files>custom_components/glt_flow_card/alarm_engine.py</files>
  <action>Fix D5's root cause. `_state_active` classifies `unavailable` and `unknown` as inactive, which makes a restart look like every alarm clearing at once — and the clear path writes `cleared`, sets `acknowledged=False` and pops `shelved_until`. Introduce a third answer, `indeterminate`, distinct from active and inactive, which holds the previous state rather than transitioning. An alarm whose entity vanished has not returned to normal; nobody has any idea what it is doing, and saying "cleared" is the one answer that is certainly wrong.</action>
  <acceptance_criteria>
    - `unavailable` and `unknown` produce `indeterminate`, not a clear.
    - Acknowledgement and shelving survive an indeterminate period.
    - A sustained indeterminate period is itself surfaced, not silently held forever.
  </acceptance_criteria>
  <verify><automated>py -3.13 -m pytest tests/components/glt_flow_card/test_alarm_restart.py -q -x</automated></verify>
  <done>A restart no longer looks like a mass clear.</done>
</task>
