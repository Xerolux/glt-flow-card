## Phase Goal

**As an** engineer, **I want** the 02:30 night setback to behave predictably on the two days a year it is strange, **so that** the plant does not silently skip a setback or run one twice.

<objective>Resolve schedules to instants, and prove both runtimes agree across DST.</objective>

<tasks>
<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: The two predicates and the resolver</name>
  <files>src/v100/schedule-time.mjs, custom_components/glt_flow_card/schedule_time.py</files>
  <action>Implement `localTimeExists(date, time, zone)` and `localTimeAmbiguous(date, time, zone)` in both runtimes — the round-trip and the fold comparison, four lines each. Home Assistant's `util/dt.py` has exactly these as `_datetime_exists` and `_datetime_ambiguous`, and they are the right semantics, but they are underscore-prefixed and can vanish in a minor release, so we implement rather than import. Build `resolveEntry(entry, date, zone)` returning the resolved UTC instants plus a declared status: `normal`, `nonexistent`, or `ambiguous` with both candidate instants. Then declare the policy for each — the resolution for a nonexistent time and which fold an ambiguous one runs in — as configuration with a documented conservative default, not as an implicit consequence of the arithmetic.</action>
  <acceptance_criteria>
    - Both predicates agree with the vendored Home Assistant's private ones over the corpus.
    - A nonexistent time returns a status, never an empty list that reads as "nothing scheduled".
    - An ambiguous time returns both candidates and the configured choice.
  </acceptance_criteria>
  <verify><automated>py -3.13 -m pytest tests/components/glt_flow_card/test_schedule_dst.py -q -x</automated></verify>
  <done>Resolution is explicit about both transitions.</done>
</task>

  <name>Task 2: Replace the wall-clock comparison and the fold-blind key</name>
  <files>custom_components/glt_flow_card/schedule_time.py, custom_components/glt_flow_card/__init__.py</files>
  <action>`run_schedules` compares `sched["time"]` against `now.strftime("%H:%M")` and keys the dedupe on `%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M`, which discards the offset. Executed against `Europe/Berlin`, the lost hour's minutes are never delivered at all, and the ambiguous hour's minutes are delivered twice with the same key — so the single execution today comes from the cache, not from the logic. Compare resolved instants, and key the dedupe on the resolved UTC instant, so the Plan 06-10 prune fix cannot reintroduce a double fire.</action>
  <acceptance_criteria>
    - A 02:30 entry on 2027-03-28 produces the declared status rather than silently not running.
    - A 02:30 entry on 2027-10-31 runs exactly once with the dedupe cache disabled.
    - No comparison of wall-clock strings remains on the execution path.
  </acceptance_criteria>
